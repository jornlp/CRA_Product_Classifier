# Reviewer worksheet — independent classification

Please classify each product or situation below under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber
Resilience Act), as it stands with Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.

The cases are in no particular order and are not grouped. Some are deliberately similar; please
treat each on its own.

## How to answer

For each row give two answers, and a note where it helps.

| Column | Allowed answers |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | `IN` · `OUT` · `UNRESOLVED` |
| **Class** | `DEFAULT` · `IMPORTANT_I` · `IMPORTANT_II` · `CRITICAL` · `UNRESOLVED` · `N/A` |

Please read these as instructions, not conventions:

- **`UNRESOLVED` is a real answer**, not a failure to answer. Use it whenever the facts given are
  insufficient, the law is genuinely unsettled, or the evidence conflicts. We are at least as
  interested in where you say UNRESOLVED as in where you commit.
- **Assume the facts stated are all the facts known.** Do not assume additional detail. If a
  decisive fact is missing, that is itself the finding.
- Assume EU market activity and a commercial context unless the row says otherwise.
- `Class` may be `N/A` where the row is about timing or scope rather than classification.
- A note on *why*, or on what you would need to know, is more valuable to us than the answer.

It is fine to work in batches; the order carries no meaning.

## Why we are asking

We have our own expected answer for every row, and we are deliberately not showing it. We want
to compare yours against ours without anchoring you. Disagreements are the point of the
exercise: they tell us where our reading is wrong, or where the question is harder than we
thought.
