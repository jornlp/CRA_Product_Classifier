# CRA reviewer worksheet — independent classification (completed)

**Basis and interpretation (12 October 2026).** Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act, CRA), Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 (technical descriptions), and relevant Commission interpretive guidance from 27 July 2026. This is an independent reviewer interpretation, not a conformity certificate or legal opinion. Each case is assessed using only its stated facts, assuming EU commercial market activity except where contradicted or explicitly made uncertain.

**Reading the columns.** `IN` means the CRA applies to the described product or actor in principle; a transition or testing rule can still reduce current obligations. `OUT` means the particular product/situation described is outside the CRA's product-market requirements. `UNRESOLVED` means a legally decisive fact is missing or ambiguous. `Class = N/A` is used for scope, actor roles, testing or timing rather than a meaningful product-category assessment. For component descriptions, the answer concerns the described marketable component rather than automatically elevating the surrounding system. References to Implementing Regulation Annex I `I.n` / `II.n` denote important Class I / Class II category numbers; Annex II denotes critical categories.
